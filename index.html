User Guide
Everything you need to get started with Family Tree
🚀 Getting Started

After logging in you'll see the Home Screen with all your saved trees.

  • Create a new tree — click + New Tree.
  • Import a GEDCOM file — drop a .ged file onto the drop zone, or click Import GEDCOM…. Your entire existing tree from another program comes in instantly.
  • Open a tree — click its card. You can have as many trees as you like.
  • Rename or delete a tree using the buttons on its card.
💡 Your data is stored securely on the server — it's available from any device that can reach this page.
👤 Adding & Editing People

Click + Person in the top bar (or press N) to add someone new.

  • Name — enter given name(s) and surname separately. Add a Nickname / Known As (e.g. "Bill" for William) if useful.
  • Sex — Male, Female, or Unknown. Used for chart colouring and pronouns.
  • Living — tick this for living people; their dates will be suppressed in GEDCOM exports for privacy.
  • Birth & Death — date and place. Use any readable format, e.g. 15 MAR 1920 or 1920.
  • Additional Events — add baptism, marriage, census, occupation, emigration, and more.
  • Photos — paste image URLs to attach photos (shown in the person's info panel).
  • Sources & Notes — record where information came from and any research notes.

To edit a person, select them in the left-hand list then click Edit or press E. To delete, use the red Delete button inside the edit form.

💡 The coloured dot beside each name in the list shows record completeness: 🟢 ≥ 80% · 🟡 ≥ 50% · 🔴 > 0% · ⚫ nothing recorded. Hover the dot to see the exact score.
🔗 Relationships

Relationships are set inside the Edit Person form. Each person can have:

  • Father & Mother — search by name and select. Sets the parent–child link.
  • Spouses / Partners — search and add. Each partnership is a separate family record and can include a marriage date and place.
  • Children — search and add any children from the current family.
  • Siblings — search to add; the app links them via shared parents automatically.

Use the 🔗 Relation button in the header (or press R) to find the relationship path between the selected person and any other person in the tree — e.g. "3rd cousin twice removed".

📊 The Views (Tabs)
1 ListFamily-group view of the selected person: their parents, siblings, own details, and their own families. Click any name to jump to that person. Click 📄 to open a printable Family Group Sheet.
2 AncestorsPedigree chart showing ancestors of the selected person. Use + / − to control how many generations are shown. Pan and zoom with mouse.
3 DescendantsDescendant chart — the selected person at the top with their descendants below. Export as SVG or PNG using the buttons at the bottom-left.
4 TimelineHorizontal timeline of births and deaths across the whole tree. Pan to explore different centuries.
5 PlacesAll places mentioned in the tree, sorted by how often they appear. Click a place to see who was born or died there.
6 SurnamesAll surnames grouped alphabetically. Click a letter to jump to it; click a surname group to see all bearers.
7 StatsStatistics: people count, sex ratio, births/deaths per decade chart, most common surnames, and pedigree collapse detection (couples who share a common ancestor).
8 FanFan (wheel) chart of ancestors. Use + / − to adjust generations. Pan and zoom.
9 MapGeographic map plotting birth and death places. Requires an internet connection for geocoding. Click a marker to see who is linked to that place.
⧗ HourglassShows ancestors above and descendants below the selected person in one connected view.
💡 Press [ / ] to cycle through tabs, or press the number key 1–9 to jump directly to a tab. Single-key shortcuts can be switched off in the Keyboard shortcuts panel.
🔍 Search & Filter

The sidebar on the left lists everyone in the tree. Use it to navigate and filter:

  • Search box — press / from anywhere to focus it, then type a name. Matches any part of the name.
  • Sex filter — restrict to Male, Female, or All.
  • Born ≥ / Born ≤ — filter by birth year range.
  • Extra — show only people who have additional events (baptism, census, etc.) recorded.
  • Dim — grey out everyone who is not a blood relative of the selected person.
  • Sort — alphabetically (A–Z), oldest first (Birth ↑), or youngest first (Birth ↓).
  • ⚯ Relation-to-all — click this button to display the relationship label from the selected person to everyone else in the list.
📥 Importing Data
  • GEDCOM (.ged) — the standard genealogy file format. Drop a file on the home screen drop zone, or click Import GEDCOM…. Compatible with Ancestry, MyHeritage, FamilySearch, Gramps, and most other programs.
  • Merge GEDCOM — open a tree, then choose More → Merge another GEDCOM to merge a second GEDCOM file into the current tree. Useful for combining two separate trees.
  • CSV spreadsheet — choose More → Import people from CSV. Columns are detected automatically. Include headings like First Name, Last Name, Sex, Birth Date, Birth Place, Death Date, Death Place, Notes.
💡 When you add someone whose name already exists, you'll be shown their birth year and relations so you can decide whether it's the same person. Use More → Find duplicates after an import to tidy up.
📤 Exporting Data
  • More → Export GEDCOM — download the whole tree as a GEDCOM 7.0 file, compatible with Ancestry, MyHeritage, FamilySearch, Gramps, and any other genealogy application.
  • SVG / PNG — export the descendant chart as a scalable vector image (SVG) or a raster image (PNG) using the buttons at the bottom-left of the Chart tab.
  • Family Group Sheet — in the List view, click the 📄 button next to any person to open a print-ready family group sheet in a new window. Shows the person's details, parents, spouses, marriage info, and a children table.
  • More → Print this view — prints the current view.

💡 Tips & Tricks
  • Completeness dots — the coloured circle beside each name rates how complete the record is, based on birth date/place, parents, sex, and (for deceased) death date/place. Aim for green!
  • Random person — click 🎲 or press X to jump to a random person. Great for discovering forgotten entries in a large tree.
  • Undo — made a mistake? Press Ctrl Z to undo the last change. Multiple levels of undo are supported.
  • Age at marriage — when you know both a person's birth year and a marriage year, the age at marriage is calculated automatically in their info panel.
  • Nicknames — the Known As field stores nicknames (stored as NICK in GEDCOM). These appear in the person's info panel alongside their formal name.
  • Pedigree collapse — in the Stats tab, the app identifies couples who share a common ancestor. This is common in historical, royal, and close-knit community trees.
  • Multiple trees — go back to the home screen (click 🌳) to switch between trees or start a new one. Trees are completely independent.
  • Date formats — dates are stored as entered. The app understands formats like 15 MAR 1920, MAR 1920, 1920, ABT 1850 (about), and BEF 1900 (before).
